Your whole desktop,
made of glass.

A glass look for GNOME, with an app to tune it from liquid to frosted. The panel, menus, apps, terminal and lock screen all get real, light-bending glass.

Version @VERSION@ · Free and open source (MIT)

Glass everywhere, not just a blur

Each panel, menu and window sits on a live, blurred copy of what's behind it, seen through a slab of glass with a rounded edge.

The whole desktop

Top bar or taskbar, menus, Quick Settings, calendar, notifications and the lock screen. GTK, libadwaita, Flatpak and Qt apps, and your terminal too.

Liquid to frosted

One slider sets the look, from clear liquid glass to soft frost. The preview updates live, and your desktop changes only when you press Apply.

Real optics

The edge bends light like real glass: refraction, a faint rainbow of dispersion, Fresnel reflection on the rim, and a little absorption at the edge.

Fits your setup

Works on GNOME as it comes. If you use Dash to Panel, Dash to Dock, Ubuntu Dock, ArcMenu or Spawn, it themes those to match.

Weather frost

If you like, the glass follows the weather: misty when it's cool, frosted when it's cold, glowing ice below freezing. Off until you turn it on.

Safe and reversible

Runs as you, with no network access. Everything is backed up first, See changes lists it before Apply, and Restore original puts it all back.

See it on a real desktop

One app to tune it

Pick a look, slide from liquid to frosted, choose a colour, and press Apply. The app looks at what you have installed and only shows the options that fit your desktop.

  • Live preview of your own wallpaper
  • A switch for every part on the Apps page
  • Updates keep your look, no Apply needed
  • Optional glass login screen

Install

Pick your Linux and paste the block into a terminal. Each one checks the signing key's fingerprint before trusting it, then installs from the signed repository. After that, your normal system update brings new versions.

k=$(mktemp) && curl -fsSLo "$k" https://djshiye.github.io/fedora-glass/RPM-GPG-KEY-fedora-glass &&
{ [ "$(gpg --show-keys --with-colons "$k" | awk -F: '$1 == "pub" { getline; print $10 }')" = D49A308168257FDBF9D5AE14BD1AC1C6F56A7252 ] || { echo "Stopped: not the Fedora Glass key."; false; }; } &&
sudo install -Dm644 "$k" /etc/pki/rpm-gpg/RPM-GPG-KEY-fedora-glass && sudo rpm --import "$k" &&
printf '[fedora-glass-setup]\nname=Fedora Glass\nbaseurl=https://djshiye.github.io/fedora-glass/rpms/\nincludepkgs=fedora-glass\ngpgcheck=1\nrepo_gpgcheck=1\ngpgkey=file:///etc/pki/rpm-gpg/RPM-GPG-KEY-fedora-glass\nmetadata_expire=1h\n' | sudo tee /etc/yum.repos.d/fedora-glass-setup.repo >/dev/null &&
sudo dnf install fedora-glass
sudo rm -f /etc/yum.repos.d/fedora-glass-setup.repo
